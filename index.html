The Golden Ritual

How to Serve at Peak Elevation

Temperature and glassware alter effervescence dynamics. Follow the Coca-Cola master standard for supreme cold satisfaction.

1

Target 3°C (37°F)

Chill the 330ml can thoroughly for at least 3 hours. True crispness unlocks just above freezing.

2

Heavy Cubed Ice

Fill a highball glass to the brim with dense, crystal-clear cubes. Avoid crushed ice to prevent premature dilution.

3

The 45° Gliding Pour

Tilt the glass and pour along the interior curve. Finish with an optional razor-thin slice of fresh lemon or lime.
